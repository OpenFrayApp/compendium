# Tome of Dark Creatures: licensing review

Status: not approved for ingestion or publication.

## Source inspected

The supplied 176-page PDF credits DragonsandStories as its author on page 2.
Its metadata contains no author, publication date, or edition identifier.
Page references below use the PDF page numbers.
The PDF remains outside the repository.

The creator’s [Dreamkin preview](https://www.patreon.com/dragonsandstories/posts/dreamkin-tome-of-79775525)
identifies the project online. Search results also surfaced third-party copies;
those do not establish permission or a newer licensing notice.

## Reuse designation

Page 2 designates unique stat blocks, unique spell descriptions, and specified
SRD-derived rules content as Open Game Content under OGL 1.0a.
That designation is expressly subject to the Product Identity declaration.

The same page reserves proper names, including every character, location, item,
and monster name unique to the book. It also reserves lore, artwork, trade dress,
and undesignated text. Stat blocks therefore cannot be imported unchanged with
all their current names and references under the stated OGL terms.

The declaration’s fair-use statement concerns references in non-open content.
It does not grant OpenFray permission to republish that content.

## License and attribution

Pages 174–175 contain OGL 1.0a, including Section 15.
The printed Section 15 lists the OGL and SRD 5.1 copyright notices only.
It does not include a copyright entry for Tome of Dark Creatures or its author.
A full-document text search found no separate book-specific copyright notice.
No Creative Commons license was found in the extracted text.

This is an incomplete attribution record for reviewing the book’s original Open
Game Content. Do not invent a copyright date or author notice to fill the gap.

## Next steps

Ask the rights holder for a corrected Section 15 notice and clarification of
whether creature names and necessary stat-block references can be reused.
Alternatively, obtain a written license covering those mechanics and names,
with the required attribution and redistribution terms.
Review any third-party references separately; the author’s permission can cover
only material for which they hold the necessary rights.

Do not build an extractor or publish creatures while these gates remain unresolved.
