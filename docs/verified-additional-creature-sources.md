# Verified additional creature-source search

## Khyberia SRD

Author: Nick Stefanski.
License: CC-BY-4.0, explicitly granted for the SRD’s contents.

Primary sources:

- [Author’s release page](https://tzarfenix.itch.io/khyberia-srd) describes its
  original rules and stat blocks as 5e-compatible and explicitly permits free
  and commercial reuse under CC-BY.
- [Author-hosted SRD PDF](https://khyberia.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Khyberia-SRD.pdf)
  gives the CC-BY-4.0 grant and required attribution on page 1.

The inspected seven-page PDF contains ten fixed-stat creature blocks:
Frogoblin, Frogoblin Chorister, Frogoblin Faerie Knight, Frogoblin Hopper,
Frogoblin Hunter, Frogoblin Princess, Muck Mephit, War Snail, Water Guardian,
and Wodyanoi.

The inspected creatures use conventional 5e fields and actions. No expertise dice,
strife, elite-monster designation, or Titanic size was found in these blocks.
The legal page does credit A5ESRD as an upstream source, alongside SRD 5.1.
That credit does not itself establish an incompatible mechanic, but it must remain
visible when evaluating the source against the requested scope.

Reuse needs the specified Nick Stefanski attribution and applicable upstream
credits, the license link, and a notice of adaptation or transcription changes.
The supplied October 2023 revision expands this collection to 21 creatures.
Its reviewed ingestion pipeline uses that 14-page PDF, not the older online file.
See [the October 2023 source review](./khyberia-review.md) for validation results
and retained source inconsistencies.

## Sources not cleared

Authorized retailer samples for the 5e editions of Faerie Bestiary and
Mediterranean Monsters were inspected. The samples confirm conventional 5e
content but omit the original-content licensing declaration and Section 15.
They are not verified reuse recommendations.

Samples inspected:

- [Faerie Bestiary 5e](https://d1vzi28wh99zvq.cloudfront.net/pdf_previews/414705-sample.pdf),
  linked through the preview product identified by
  [Paizo’s announcement](https://paizo.com/blog/the-ultimate-faeries-kickstarter-from-legendary-games-ends-today).
- [Mediterranean Monsters](https://d1vzi28wh99zvq.cloudfront.net/pdf_previews/485463-sample.pdf).

Nord’s Ultimate Bestiary titles and Fifth Edition Foes remain off the reuse
shortlist. Their complete, edition-specific grants were not verified in this work.
No new large 5e/2024 bestiary was cleared by this follow-up search.
