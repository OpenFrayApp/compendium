# GM Binder creature-source search

## Result

No substantial, standalone DnD 5e or Basic Rules 2024 bestiary was verified for
reuse under CC-BY, OGL, or ORC in this search. This is a bounded search result,
not a claim that none exists on GM Binder.

Two documents explicitly license their own text under CC-BY-4.0, but their creature
material depends on player-character features. Neither is recommended for the
current fixed-stat creature pipeline. No dataset was imported.

## Explicitly licensed material

### Necromancer (Full Caster), by somanyrobots

[Document](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-OJzCKCXLroIdcTDqOCK).
Its Licensing section grants CC-BY-4.0 for all rules and spell text, separately
from its attribution to the SRD and other spell authors.

Appendix 1 contains necromantic minion stat blocks, including Zombie Minion,
Shadow Minion, Corpse Colossus, and Specter Minion.
The appendix explicitly uses the summoner’s proficiency bonus, Intelligence
modifier, spell attack modifier, and spirit-point cost in the statistics.
These are class-dependent companions, not a fixed-stat bestiary.
The current source display also names a soul-point cost; the class mechanics use
spirit points. Treat that terminology as a source-review issue.

Do not silently substitute arbitrary character statistics or add a build model
for this source. Artwork is outside the reviewed rules-text grant.

### 5e++, by KibblesTasty

[Document](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-Nx9KikmtGvMJUoPx3p_).
Its Creative Commons Usage section explicitly licenses the document’s text under
CC-BY-4.0 and excludes artwork. This is separate from its SRD attribution.

The document is a revised rules and player-options system, not a bestiary.
Its Beast Companion and Dragon Form blocks depend on character features.
It does not meet the current DnD-only fixed-stat library scope.

## Collections without verified reuse coverage

- [Monstrous Compendiums, by heathsmith](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-Newt4vNKhqBgzcsYAUY):
  a large creature collection. No license, copyright, Open Game, or Creative
  Commons declaration was found in the fetched readable document. Author
  permission or another authoritative notice is needed.
- [The Book of Hordes, by aeyana](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-LZWXq83Rk2jZNs7spok):
  includes horde statistics. No reuse license was found in the fetched document.
- [Psychic and Spiritual Handbook, by Therin Creative](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-N3Ic3oDJCMCfNp1fddO):
  includes a bestiary, but describes itself as unofficial Fan Content. Its free-use
  language does not establish one of the requested licenses.
- [The Twisted Artist, by RyRiann](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-NfGBc3eTcHDLe9FRSkh):
  a single creature, not a compendium. Its OGL appendix designates the SRD as Open
  Game Content and lists only the OGL and SRD notices. It does not establish a
  complete, author-specific designation and attribution record for this creature.

## False positives

- [Abomination, by somanyrobots](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-OaIBtALWJfcfEV3_vcb)
  explicitly licenses its rules and spell text under CC-BY-4.0, but is a player
  class, not a creature book.
- CC-BY references in some results license only artwork or the underlying SRD.
  They do not establish a grant for all original creature text.
- Searches for ORC also return references to orc creatures and languages.
  No eligible 5e bestiary with an actual ORC notice was verified.
- Monster Hunter, Warcraft, and official-setting fan conversions require separate
  rights checks. Free access and an SRD notice do not license those franchises.
- CC-BY-NC and CC-BY-NC-SA are different licenses from CC-BY-4.0 and were not
  treated as matches.

## Recommendation

The most useful permission leads are Monstrous Compendiums and The Book of Hordes.
Request a grant covering original creature mechanics and names, plus the necessary
attribution and any upstream reuse restrictions, before building an ingest.
